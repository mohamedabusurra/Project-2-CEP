## Primary — law and government

1. **European Commission.** (2026, July 20). *Guidelines on transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems (Article 50, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).* Shaping Europe's Digital Future.
   https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-transparency-ai-generated-content
   — Article 50 applicability date (2 August 2026), the four disclosure duties, marking and labelling obligations.

2. **Federal Trade Commission.** (2026, May 19). *FTC begins enforcing the TAKE IT DOWN Act* [Press release].
   https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/05/ftc-begins-enforcing-take-it-down-act
   — 19 May 2026 compliance deadline, 48-hour removal requirement, warning letters to 15 named platforms.

3. **Federal Bureau of Investigation, Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3).** (2026, April). *Annual report, 2025 data.*
   — First use of an AI-related crime descriptor: 22,364 complaints, US$893.35M in adjusted losses.

4. **Hong Kong Police Force / Arup public statement.** (2024, May), as reported by CNN and the Financial Times.
   — The US$25.6M synthetic video-conference fraud; 15 transfers to 5 accounts.

## Industry research and datasets

5. **Surfshark.** (2026, April). *Global deepfake fraud reaches $2.19B — US leads in losses.* Compiled from the AI Incident Database, Resemble.AI and OECD records, January 2019 – March 2026.
   — US$2.19B documented global losses; US$1.65B in 2025 alone; US share US$712M; social media as largest origin.

6. **Entrust.** (2025, November 18). *2026 Identity Fraud Report.* Based on 1B+ identity verifications across 195 countries.
   — Deepfakes now drive 1 in 5 biometric fraud attempts.

7. **iProov.** (2025). *Threat Intelligence Report 2025.*
   — Only 0.1% of participants reliably distinguished real from AI-generated content.

8. **Europol.** (2025). *Internet Organised Crime Threat Assessment (IOCTA) 2025.*
   — Projection of ~8 million deepfakes shared online in 2025, against ~500,000 in 2023.

9. **FINRA.** (2026). *Regulatory Oversight Report.*
   — AI-generated voices and deepfake threats named explicitly; risk-based compliance programmes required of member firms.

10. **MultiState.** (2026). *Deepfake legislation tracker.*
    — 47 US states, ~169 deepfake-related laws enacted since 2022.

## Consulted but not relied on

The following were read during research and are listed for transparency. We did **not** use their headline growth percentages, because the figures are measured over inconsistent windows, are frequently forecasts presented as measurements, and contradict one another across vendors: aggregated "deepfake statistics 2026" pages from Keepnet Labs, DeepStrike/StationX, Scam.ai, Digital Applied and Adaptive Security; and Deloitte's US$40B-by-2027 generative-AI fraud projection.

Where these pages pointed to a named primary dataset (Entrust, iProov, IC3, Surfshark, Europol), we went to that source instead and cited it directly. Reasoning is written up in §8 of `research-notes/research-material.md`.
